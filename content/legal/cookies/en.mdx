## Summary

This website uses no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and it loads no resources from other websites: the fonts are served from the site itself. That is why it shows no cookie banner.

## What is stored in your browser

- **NEXT_LOCALE** (first-party cookie). Remembers the language you chose. It lasts as long as the browser session.
- **tema** (local storage). Remembers whether you prefer light or dark mode. It stays until you delete it.

Both are needed to provide the service you ask for (viewing the site in your language and theme), so they do not require consent under Article 22(2) of the LSSI-CE. They are not sent to third parties.

## How to delete them

You can delete cookies and site data from your browser settings. The next time you visit, the site will pick the default language and theme again.

## Third-party websites

Links to GitHub, LinkedIn and demos hosted on Vercel take you to websites with their own cookies and policies.

## Changes

If I ever add analytics or other non-essential cookies, I will ask for your consent before using them and update this page.
