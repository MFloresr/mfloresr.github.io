---
revisado: true
titulo: Sudokus
resumen: Web sudoku game with user accounts, saved games, statistics and an in-house engine that generates and grades every puzzle.
contexto: null
objetivo: Offer a complete web sudoku where each player saves their games and tracks their progress.
problema: Many online sudokus don't save progress, have poorly calibrated difficulty or allow cheating. Here every game is saved to the account, difficulty is calculated by solving the puzzle the way a person would, and every move is validated on the server.
usuario: Anyone who wants to play sudoku on their phone or computer and track their progress.
estadoTexto: Released in September 2026 with 424 sudokus (237 easy, 127 medium and 60 hard), all generated by its own engine, and 27 automated tests.
funcionalidades:
  - Board that highlights the row, column, box and matching numbers.
  - On-screen keypad showing the remaining numbers, plus physical keyboard support (numbers, arrows, N for notes, Delete, Ctrl+Z).
  - Pencil notes, undo, erase, hints, a timer with pause and 3 lives.
  - Every move is validated on the server; the solution never reaches the browser.
  - Games saved per user, with continue and retry.
  - Victory screen (time, mistakes, hints and best time) and defeat screen.
  - List by status and difficulty with thumbnails; new random game among the unplayed ones.
  - 'Profile with stats: solved, day streak, win rate, best time and average by difficulty.'
  - Sign-up, email login and password recovery.
  - In-house engine that solves (unique solution), grades difficulty and generates sudokus.
retos:
  - titulo: Grading difficulty objectively
    texto: Instead of counting clues, a solver applies human techniques (singles, locked candidates, pairs) and sets the level by the hardest technique it needs.
  - titulo: 100% original content
    texto: Every sudoku is generated by the engine with a verified unique solution; puzzles from an external dataset were dropped because their license couldn't be guaranteed.
  - titulo: No cheating, no conflicts
    texto: The solution lives only on the server, each move locks the game in the database and there can't be two ongoing games of the same sudoku.
  - titulo: Secure credentials
    texto: The database password was generated locally and only its SCRAM hash was sent to Supabase.
mejoras:
  - Add more advanced techniques to the engine to offer an expert level.
  - Add more games to the platform.
arquitectura:
  nodos:
    - titulo: Browser
      detalle: Alpine.js · board, notes and keypad
    - titulo: Django
      detalle: 'views + JSON API · engine: solve, grade and generate'
      destacado: true
    - titulo: PostgreSQL
      detalle: Supabase · dedicated schema and role
  nota: Django is deployed on Vercel (London). Each sudoku's solution never leaves the server.
